About the venue

in the heart of the former Gdańsk Shipyard.

02

FAQ

Short answers to the questions we get most often. The rest — in the regulations.

Until when do I have to fill out the declaration and what do I need to provide?

The declaration form must be completed by May 13th (Wednesday). We want you to make a final decision at this stage regarding whether you are attending the event with a plus one or opting for a single ticket.

What are the fees and what does the payment schedule look like?

The total cost of participation will be approximately 500 PLN (we assume fluctuations of +/- 25 PLN). We will split the amount into 3 installments of similar size (up to approx. 170 PLN). The first payment deadline is May 15th, the second by the end of June.

Who should I pay the money to?

To make it easier, fees will be collected in groups by the group representatives (starości), who will then pass them on to the appropriate members of the organizing committee.

What will happen to the leftover change and small surpluses?

Unused small change (estimated 1.50 - 2.00 PLN per person) will not be refunded for logistical reasons. The entire pool will be donated to charity organizations.

Who can participate and does a non-university person pay the same?

The event is for GUMed students (halfway through their studies) and their guests. The cost for a plus one is the same as for a student – the fee covers the exact same package of benefits.

When do I need to provide the final details of my plus one?

We will collect the first and last name of your plus one at the turn of July and August in a separate form.

What if there is a date collision with an Erasmus+ trip?

If you are planning a trip, you must decide: a short return to Gdańsk for this weekend or resignation. We advise checking the host university's calendar, as the decision to pay is binding.

Where and when is the Halfway Party taking place?

Saturday, October 17th, Twoja Stocznia (4 Elektryków St.). The date and location are now final and will not change.

What do I get in the ticket price?

Entry, open bar, catering, music (DJ), photo coverage, and decorations.

Can I bring my own alcohol?

No. All alcohol is served by the staff as part of the open bar. Bringing your own bottles is strictly prohibited.

What is the dress code?

Elegant – a shirt or blazer is welcome. Note: the venue is post-industrial (uneven surfaces), so you wear heels at your own risk!

Will there be a cloakroom and safe transport?

A cloakroom will be available (may be paid). The venue is 10 mins from SKM Gdańsk Stocznia. We recommend public transport or apps like Uber/Bolt.

Will the photos be posted online?

Yes, entry implies consent to the publication of the photo coverage on social media. If you do not want your image published, report it to the photographer on site.

What if the event is canceled?

We refund the amounts paid minus documented, non-refundable costs already incurred (e.g., advances for subcontractors).

Will it be possible to dance with the Dean?

Regulations

The full text of the Event regulations. Acceptance of the regulations is a condition of participation.

§1

Nature of the Event

  1. The event "GUMed Students' Halfway Party" (hereinafter: "Event") is a private closed party, organized by the students' social committee (hereinafter: "Organizer").
  2. The Organizer is not an entrepreneur or a professional mass event/tourism organizer. The event is based on the mutual trust of the participants.
  3. The facility where the Event takes place is managed by an external entity (hereinafter: "Facility Operator"). The Organizer only rents the space and coordinates logistics.
§2

Participant Liability

  1. The Participant declares that they are aware of the post-industrial nature of the "Twoja Stocznia" facility (possible uneven terrain, thresholds, steel structures) and undertakes to exercise extreme caution.
  2. Full liability: The Participant takes part in the Event at their own exclusive risk. The Organizer is not liable for any personal injury (accidents, injuries, health impairment) or property damage that occurs on the premises.
  3. Release from liability: By accepting the regulations, the Participant irrevocably waives any claims for damages against natural persons comprising the Organizing Committee.
  4. The Participant bears full civil and criminal liability for their actions, including damage to the hall's infrastructure, sound equipment, or decorations.
§3

Security and Order

  1. The Facility Operator is exclusively responsible for the technical safety of the hall, fire installations, and structures.
  2. Professional security guards watch over order. The Participant is obliged to strictly follow the instructions of the security and the Organizer.
  3. Refusal of entry / Removal: Security or the Organizer may remove from the Event premises any person who:
    • behaves aggressively or is a nuisance to others;
    • destroys property;
    • creates a threat to themselves or others (e.g., attempts to climb structural elements of the hall).
  4. A removed person is not entitled to a refund of paid fees.
§4

Alcohol and Catering

  1. On the Event premises, alcohol is served in the "Open Bar" system or by the Facility Operator's bartenders.
  2. Exclusion of fault: The Organizer is not responsible for the quality of alcohol, food, and the consequences of their consumption (including poisoning, alcohol intoxication).
  3. The Organizer and bar staff reserve the right to refuse serving alcohol to an intoxicated person.
  4. Bringing your own alcohol, glass bottles, and intoxicating substances is prohibited.
§5

Liability for Guests

  1. Every GUMed student inviting a plus one (from outside the university) bears full financial and legal responsibility for them.
  2. In the event of damage caused by a plus one, the Organizer has the right to charge the repair costs directly to the inviting student.
§6

Cloakroom and Private Property

  1. The cloakroom may be paid or free, managed by the Facility Operator or third parties.
  2. The Organizer is not responsible for items left in the cloakroom, on tables, in booths, or in restrooms (phones, wallets, clothing).
  3. Found items should be handed over to security or the Organizer.
§7

Image

  1. Entry to the "Twoja Stocznia" hall is tantamount to consenting to the free recording of one's image by the official Event photographers and its publication on the Internet (Social Media).
  2. A Participant who does not consent to the publication of a photo in which they are clearly visible is obliged to report this directly to the photographer or Organizer during the event.
§8

Force Majeure and Event Cancellation

  1. The Organizer reserves the right to change the date or cancel the Event for reasons beyond their control (e.g., administrative decision, hall failure, national mourning, sanitary restrictions).
  2. In the event of cancellation, the Organizer will refund the amounts paid to participants minus documented, non-refundable costs already incurred (e.g., an unrecoverable advance for the club).
§9

Final Provisions

  1. Ignorance of the regulations does not exempt one from complying with them.
  2. Any contentious issues not included in this document are ultimately resolved by the Organizer in consultation with Security and the Facility Operator.
  3. The governing law for disputes is Polish law, and the competent court is the District Court in Gdańsk.
